Environmental Benefits

Small footprint

The plant is effectively mobile, and is not a permanent structure onsite.  Its footprint is small, especially in comparison to other sand processing plants in use in the Northern Territory.

On site there are minimal modifications to the natural environment.

Recycling Water

The plant significantly reduces water consumption by using a system called Aquacycle. The use of this system brings significant environmental benefits as it reduces the area previously required to accommodate settling ponds and also reduces the volume of fresh water required by up to 90%.

This is an exciting new method for how extractive operators in the Northern Territory can introduce a washing plant to their processes, as it effectively tackles the issues around water availability and usage.

The only water that will not be re-used is the water that will remain in the sand product as moisture content.

Most sand plants are huge water users, and here in the Northern Territory none have the capacity to re-use or recycle water.

In this plant the overflow containing the waste water is discharged to Aquacycle.

This allows for 90% of the water used during the sand washing process to be recycled to the washing plant, reducing the volume of fresh water required to feed the plant.

The Aquacycle separates waste water from the fine material with the assistance of a flocculent.

Working in conjunction with the plant’s sand washing system, this process ensures the end results are of a consistently high quality.

This state-of-the-art plant also has minimal noise output that is unlikely to be detected off site.
