ABOUT US

DRQ Products

In a first for the Northern Territory, Darwin River Quarries uses cutting edge technology to produce High Quality Sand Products that meet specific Project Specifications and Government requirements.

Our product has been extensively tested and results show that it will meet Northern Territory Government Specifications and Australian Standards for concrete aggregate.

Our Sand Resource is only 45 minutes from Darwin.  We use a state of the art washing and screening plant to produce materials that exceed individual project specifications.

Darwin River Quarries can:

  • Produce a wide range of Specialist Sand Products for Industrial Construction projects;
  • Serve the growing market for Quality Construction Materials in Darwin and surrounding areas;
  • Produce sand that meets NT Government specifications and Australian Standards for concrete aggregate for the Construction Industry.

All Products

State of the art Washing and Screening Plant

Our Sand Resource is located south of Darwin near Acacia and has convenient access to the nearby Stuart Highway to ensure year round, all weather transport access.

Our Sand Resource is not located in a low lying water course or river bed.  This is a unique situation for Northern Territory sand extraction.

Our Plant also has many positive environmental features, including a small footprint for minimal impact and it recycles up to 90% of water used dramatically reducing consumption.
